How it works

  1. Press Start Camera.
  2. Allow camera permission.
  3. Point the camera at an object.
  4. Press Capture Photo.
  5. The captured image is sent to the AI model.
  6. YOLO detects objects and draws bounding boxes.
  7. Object names and confidence are displayed.

Model: YOLO11n ONNX
